# Booking protocol

Before a payment method is saved in the browser: research + propose + hold the final step.
After: research + propose + book on the owner's yes.

Restaurants and venues
1. Read preferences.md (area, budget, cuisines, dietary) and people.md (who's coming, their dislikes).
2. Find 2 options with the built-in browser (maps + the venue's own site). One line each: name, vibe,
   price band, distance, and whether online booking exists.
3. The owner picks. If online booking exists and a payment method is saved → one confirmation line
   (CLAUDE.md money rules), book, log, calendar event with address and booking reference.
   If phone-only → say so and offer to draft a message.

Appointments (vet, dentist, accountant, trades): same flow. Prefer the regulars in people.md.
Add travel time as its own calendar block if it's over 20 minutes.

Travel: defaults in preferences.md. Always show the total price including bags and seats before asking.

Never
- Enter card details or passwords. Create accounts. Book outside the hours in preferences.md without an
  explicit "yes, outside hours".
