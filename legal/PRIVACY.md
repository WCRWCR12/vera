# Privacy Policy: Vera

**DRAFT. Not yet reviewed by a lawyer. See legal/README.md.**

Last updated: 30 October 2026

This policy explains how System GC (Queensland, Australia)
("System GC", "we", "us") handles information in connection with Vera, a free, open-source AI assistant
configuration that runs inside Claude Code on your own computer.

We are bound by the *Privacy Act 1988* (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) to the extent
they apply to us, and we follow them for everything in this policy whether or not they strictly apply.

## 1. The short version
- **Vera sends us nothing.** There are no usage statistics, analytics, crash reports or tracking in Vera.
  We do not receive your notes, tasks, messages, contacts, calendar, emails or account details.
- Vera works by using third-party services (Anthropic for Claude; Telegram, Google or Microsoft only if you
  connect them). Information goes directly from your computer to them, under their own terms.

## 2. What stays on your computer
Everything Vera stores (tasks, people, preferences, ideas, wellbeing notes, logs, Vera Screen's data) is in
your Vera folder on your computer. We have no access to it. You can delete it at any time by deleting the
folder.

## 3. Information you give us directly
If you email us, open an issue on the repository, or ask for help, we receive what you send (for example
your name, email address and the details of your question). We use it to reply, keep it only as long as we
need for that and our legal obligations, and handle it under the APPs. Anything you post publicly on the
repository (issues, discussions) is public; don't include personal details there.

## 4. Third-party services Vera uses
When you use Vera, information goes directly from your computer to these services, not through System GC:
- **Anthropic (Claude and Claude Code):** what you say to Vera and the files and pages Vera reads to answer
  you are processed by Anthropic to produce Claude's responses. Anthropic is based in the United States. Its
  terms and privacy policy apply to your Claude account.
- **Telegram** (if you set up the bot): messages, photos and voice notes sent to or from the bot.
- **Google or Microsoft** (if you connect your calendar or email in the Claude app).
- **Websites** Vera visits for you, and sites you are logged in to.

Each provider handles that information under its own terms and privacy policy. We are not responsible for
their practices, but Vera's default settings keep what is shared to what each task needs.

## 5. Security
Vera's local settings block it from reading common secrets on your computer (keys, saved passwords,
browser cookie stores) and keep the Telegram token out of chats. See SECURITY.md. No system is perfectly
secure.

## 6. Access, correction and complaints
To ask what we hold about you (only what you sent us directly), to correct it or to complain, email
**info@systemgc.com.au**. We will respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you can complain
to the **Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC)**: www.oaic.gov.au, phone 1300 363 992.

## 7. Children
Vera is not intended for people under 18.

## 8. Changes to this policy
If we change this policy we will publish the new version in this repository and change the date at the
top. We will not add any collection of information to Vera without saying so clearly first.
